## About the game

Ignite is a fast arcade game about keeping a living fire alive. Start with a tiny ember, raise the heat with taps or clicks, and reveal all seven stages of the scene. The hotter the fire becomes, the faster it cools and the more dangerous its temporary hazards become.

Reaching Inferno is only the beginning: hold the flame, improve your best score, complete eight permanent goals, and chase the next personal milestone. Every stage and record is available without ads. You may voluntarily watch a rewarded ad for ×2 heat power during 60 seconds of active play.

## Short description

Grow an ember into Inferno, hold the heat, and beat your records.

## How to play

Tap or click the fire to increase heat and score. Heat falls faster at higher stages. Watch the warnings from the Ash Servant and Ember Warden: their effects cool the fire faster, but the controls stay the same.

Reach Inferno and hold it for as long as possible. After a run, choose “Try again” to improve your records and complete the next goal. “Watch an ad — ×2 heat power for 60 seconds” appears upon reaching stage three and is entirely optional. Gameplay timers stop while the game is paused or an ad is open.

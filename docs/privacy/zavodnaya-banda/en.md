No account is required. Levels, records, nuts, rewards, language and settings are saved on your device. There is no cloud transfer between devices. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes this progress.

Optional ads

Yandex Mobile Ads SDK provides ads for hints, extra time or doubled nuts. It starts when you request an ad after allowing ads to load. You can continue normal play without this permission. Hints are free in the first three levels.

When loading and displaying ads, Yandex may process your IP address, device, operating system and app information, technical identifiers, connection details, impressions, clicks and technical errors. These are used to deliver and measure ads, confirm rewards, diagnose problems and prevent invalid impressions. Your approximate region may be inferred from your IP address.

This build disables SDK personalization and location tracking and enables age-restricted ad mode. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions or Android Advertising ID. These settings do not mean the ad network receives no technical data.

Yandex privacy policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/ — explains processing, retention and how to contact Yandex about data it receives. The game developer does not receive a copy of your advertising profile. If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and message to respond.

Your choices

Menu → Settings → Ads and privacy → Withdraw permission prevents new ad requests until you allow them again. It does not erase data already processed by Yandex. For game and data questions, email support. For ad-network data, use the contact methods in Yandex's privacy policy.

Test builds display test ads. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
